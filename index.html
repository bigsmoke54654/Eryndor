After the crash, Commander Elias wakes in a forest that is always between night and day. Memories are matter here. Something ancient is feeding on them.

II · The forest between

Everything here is a memory. Everything here is hungry.

The sky has been this colour for as long as anyone remembers. There is no day and no night, only the seam between them. The trees are not quite trees. The fruit does not taste like fruit.

III · The soul and the body

Here, soul and body are no longer the same thing.

The body wants to live. It is hungry, and afraid.

The soul remembers. It knows some things. Not everything.

IV · The Who

You hear them before you see them.

“They were not made. They arrived. And when everything they arrived for was gone, they remained, because a heart does not stop beating simply because there is no longer a reason to.”

Varr’veyr elder

Three forgotten colossi, guardians of a people that no longer exists. The instrument they carry is not an instrument. It is the heart. Five hands sustain it. The sixth points the way.

V · Six reflections

Six versions of one man, each shaped by what he could not carry.

They are made of memory, and memory cannot be killed. Stand before one. Wait. Something has to give.

VI · Three tribes. One language, earned.
Those who drowned

Shae’vaar

A city beneath the river, hidden under a ceiling of lost memories. They build upward, never outward, because width is easy to find. They worship what frightens them and believe it keeps them alive.

Bring them what they ask for. Earn the first third of the old tongue.

Those who burned

Varr’raeth

Masters of survival, hidden in the desert behind a mirage. They ran as far and as hot as they could, sure that nothing would follow. They have not looked outside their borders in an eternity.

Move your light through the haze. Earn the second third.

Those who blend

Varr’veyr

The largest tribe. They change their shape, their colour, the very consistency of their souls, and they stood against the hunger and lived. They do not ask you to prove yourself.

“We know you. We remember.” They teach the rest.

VII · Creatures of the river

The river never creates life. It only remembers it incorrectly.

Most of what lives here is a failed copy of something from another world. Observers rarely agree on what they saw. Open a field record.
